Why the IT Field Suits Me

ICT @ HZ University of Applied Sciences

I have to be honest: I am dependent on my computer. Games, videos, browsing — I spend a lot of time in front of a screen. Some people might call it an addiction, and they would not be entirely wrong. But instead of fighting it, I want to turn it into something useful.

That is why I chose the ICT programme at HZ. I want to convert my screen time into real skills and eventually into a career. If I am going to be online anyway, I might as well build things, solve problems, and one day earn money doing what I already love: being at the computer.

What Drives Me

  • Turning my passion for computers into a professional skill set
  • Learning how software and systems actually work
  • Building a future career in IT, maybe in cybersecurity
  • Showing that motivation matters more than experience
