Welcome to My ICT Portfolio

Programme & Career Orientation - HZ University of Applied Sciences

This website is my showcase project for the Programme & Career Orientation course. It is my opinion on why I believe the ICT programme fits me, built with the HTML and CSS skills I picked up in the first weeks of the course. I wanted the site to be simple, easy to navigate and honest about where I am right now: a beginner who is motivated to learn.

I spend a lot of my free time behind a screen anyway, so studying ICT is my way of turning that habit into an actual skill. On this site you can read more about who I am, why I chose IT, how my first year is planned out, and follow my progress through the blog.

What You Will Find Here

  • Profile - a short introduction about who I am
  • Why IT? - my motivation for choosing this programme
  • Dashboard - my study progress and planning for this year
  • Blog - posts about my goals, experience and direction
