Why IT?

My motivation for studying ICT

The Honest Reason

I spend a lot of time on my computer. Gaming, watching videos, browsing the internet. I enjoy it. Instead of seeing it only as a bad habit, I think it shows that technology is something I naturally like. The ICT programme gives me a way to use that in a good way.

My goal is to turn something I already do a lot into a real skill. I want to go from just using technology to creating it, and maybe make money from it later.

What I Know About IT

IT is a big field. There is web development, cybersecurity, data, AI and more. Right now cybersecurity seems interesting to me, but I have not decided yet.

IT is not only about coding. You also need problem solving, teamwork and the ability to keep learning new things.

What I Want to Learn

  • Programming and software engineering basics
  • How networks and security work
  • Build projects I can show later
  • Find out if cybersecurity is really for me
  • Turn my computer time into a real career

My Expectations

I know it will be hard sometimes. That is okay. I am ready to learn, ask for help when I need it, and keep trying even when something does not work.
