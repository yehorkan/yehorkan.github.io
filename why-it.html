Why IT?

My motivations, expectations, and what I hope to achieve in the ICT field.

The Honest Reason

I spend a lot of time on my computer — probably more than I should. Gaming, watching content, exploring the internet — it is something I genuinely enjoy. Rather than treating it as a bad habit I need to quit, I see it as a signal: technology is where I naturally gravitate. The ICT programme gives me a path to channel that energy productively.

My goal is simple: turn something I am already deeply invested in into a skill that has real value. I want to go from consuming technology to creating it — and eventually, to making a living from it.

What I Know About the Field

IT is one of the fastest-growing industries in the world. From web development to cybersecurity, from data analysis to artificial intelligence — the possibilities are enormous. What attracts me most right now is cybersecurity, though I have not fully decided on a specialisation yet.

I also know that IT is not just about coding. It requires problem-solving, teamwork, continuous learning, and the ability to adapt when things change — which happens constantly in this field.

What I Want to Learn & Achieve

  • Build a solid foundation in programming and software engineering
  • Understand how networks, systems, and security work
  • Develop real projects I can show to future employers
  • Explore cybersecurity and find out if it is the right direction for me
  • Graduate with practical skills, not just theoretical knowledge
  • Turn my passion for computers into a stable, meaningful career

My Expectations

I expect the ICT programme to be challenging — and that is exactly what I want. I am ready to fail, learn, ask for help, and try again. I do not need to be the smartest person in the room; I just need to be the one who keeps going.
