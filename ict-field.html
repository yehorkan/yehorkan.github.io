What I Know About the ICT Field

5 September 2026

Before starting the programme, "working in IT" was a pretty vague idea for me: mostly people typing code in dark-themed editors. Looking into it more, the field turns out to be much broader than I thought.

Roles I didn't know existed

  • Software developer - builds and maintains applications and websites
  • Security analyst / pentester - tests and defends systems against attacks
  • System / network administrator - keeps servers and networks running
  • Data analyst - turns raw data into insights companies can act on
  • UX/UI designer - designs how software looks and feels to use

What surprised me most is how much of the work is not just "coding alone", but also communicating with clients, colleagues and other teams to figure out what actually needs to be built or fixed. The Netherlands also has a well-known shortage of ICT professionals, which makes it feel like a relatively safe field to specialize in.

For now this only scratches the surface, but it already gives me a better picture of where I might end up, and it lines up with what I wrote about in My Direction So Far.
