Goals: A SWOT Analysis

1 September 2026

Before the programme started, I filled out the WHO AM I questionnaire on SKC. Looking back at my answers, I turned them into a small SWOT analysis of where I stand right now.

Strengths

  • I am genuinely curious about how computers and software work
  • I already spend a lot of time online, so I am comfortable with technology
  • I don't give up quickly when something doesn't work the first time

Weaknesses

  • I have very little formal programming experience
  • I sometimes get distracted by games instead of finishing my work
  • My planning and time management still need work

Opportunities

  • The IT field is growing, with a lot of demand for junior developers
  • HZ offers guidance, workshops and teacher assistants I can use
  • Group work and code reviews let me learn from fellow students

Threats

  • Falling behind if I don't keep up with the two-week course pattern
  • Losing motivation if I don't see results quickly enough
  • Spending screen time on games instead of study without noticing

My goal for this year is to turn the strengths and opportunities into real progress, while actively working on the weaknesses before they become a problem.
