Goals SWOT Analysis

August 2026 · ← Back to blog

Before starting the ICT programme, I did a SWOT analysis to understand where I stand and what I need to focus on. This helps me set realistic goals for my first year.

Strengths
  • Strong interest in technology and computers
  • Problem-solving skills from gaming
  • Team experience from volleyball and football
  • High motivation to learn and improve
Weaknesses
  • Limited formal programming experience
  • Tendency to spend too much time gaming
  • Still adapting to studying in English
  • Perfectionism can slow me down
Opportunities
  • HZ ICT programme with hands-on projects
  • Growing demand in IT and cybersecurity
  • International environment to improve English
  • Access to teachers, workshops, and peer support
Threats
  • Competitive field with experienced students
  • Risk of procrastination due to gaming habits
  • Study Advice boundary (45 EC) if I fall behind
  • Fast pace of technology requires constant learning

What I Will Do About It

My plan is to use my strengths — curiosity and motivation — to overcome my weaknesses. I will set limits on gaming during study weeks, ask for help early when I get stuck, and use the dashboard on this site to track my progress throughout the year.
