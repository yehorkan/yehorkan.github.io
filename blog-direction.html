Finding My Direction in IT

August 2026 · ← Back to blog

One of the biggest questions I have at the start of the ICT programme is: which direction should I go? IT is a huge field, and choosing a specialisation too early might close doors — but not choosing at all might mean missing opportunities.

Why Cybersecurity Interests Me

Right now, cybersecurity is the area that excites me the most. I have always been curious about how systems can be protected — and how they can be broken. Games like hacking simulations and stories about data breaches made me wonder: how does this actually work in the real world?

Cybersecurity combines technical skills with problem-solving and constant learning, which fits my personality. It also feels meaningful — protecting people's data and systems matters.

But I Am Not Fully Decided

I am keeping an open mind. Software engineering, web development, and data are also interesting to me. The first year of the ICT programme is broad by design, and I plan to use that time to explore different areas before committing to a specialisation.

My Approach

  • Pay attention in every course, not just security-related ones
  • Talk to teachers and older students about their experiences
  • Build small projects in different areas to see what I enjoy most
  • Revisit this decision at the end of the first year
