My Direction So Far

8 September 2026

A few weeks into the programme, ICT still feels like a huge field. Web development, data, AI, networking, cybersecurity - there is a lot to choose from, and I don't think I need to decide right now. Still, one direction keeps pulling my attention: cybersecurity.

I like the idea of thinking like an attacker in order to defend a system properly. It combines the puzzle-solving I enjoy in games with something that actually matters, since real people and companies depend on their systems being secure. I'm keeping an open mind for the other courses too, but if I had to bet on where I'll end up specializing, this would be it.

What I want to explore next

  • How networks are built and secured, once I get to the networking course
  • Basic concepts of ethical hacking and responsible disclosure
  • Whether cybersecurity fits my strengths better than software development does

I'll revisit this post later in the year to see if this direction held up, or if something else grabbed my interest instead.
